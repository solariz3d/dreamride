# THE PROGRESSIONS: harmony written by judgment, never generated (src/compose/harmony.rs reads this).
#
# Written 2026-11-03, after the loops species' chord root, cycling by mod(c + m, 5) out of step with the bar, was found
# not to sound good most of the time, and not because the sound was cheap: the progression was the problem. The one
# generated piece judged alright (citypop_v4) ran on real functional harmony. So every 12-tone species
# now plays one of these progressions, and its own maths writes only the melodies, rhythms and shapes on top.
#
# Format. `== name | mode | phrase P | energy E | density D | mood words`, one `#` line saying what it is, then the eight
# bars of one stage: `| chord | chord chord | ... |`, one chord a bar or two (each then takes half the bar), `%` holding
# the chord before it. The mode is the tonic's scale and the melody's: major, minor, dorian, lydian, mixolydian.
#
# Chords are Roman numerals on the tonic: upper case major, lower case minor; `b` and `#` move the root a half step;
# numerals count the major scale in major, lydian and mixolydian, the natural minor in minor and dorian (so VI in minor
# is the flat sixth). Qualities: upper case `maj7 maj9 6 69 add9 7 9 13 7b9 7#9 7sus 9sus + °`, lower case
# `7 9 11 6 m6 maj7 ø7 °7 °`. `/3`, `/5`, `/7` put the chord's third, fifth or seventh in the bass (an inversion, written
# on purpose). A chord ending `!` is a marked deceptive turn: it stands where the tonic was due.
#
# The rules every progression keeps (tests/harmony.rs proves them): it starts on the tonic and its last chord is a
# dominant (V of any colour, V7sus, viiø7 or vii°7, the tritone substitute bII7, the backdoor bVII7), so every stage
# seam is a cadence whatever comes next; each phrase (4 or 8 bars) ends on a dominant and the next begins on the tonic
# unless that chord is marked `!`; chords change only on bar lines and half bars.
