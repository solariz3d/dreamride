# SPECIES: TRITAVE. Bohlen-Pierce tuning in 12/8, odd harmonics only (src/compose/book.rs).
#
# The Bohlen-Pierce scale divides the tritave (3:1, an octave and a fifth) into 13 equal steps, bp(k) = Tk + 12 k
# log2(3) / 13, about 1.463 semitones each. It has no octave. Its chords are the odd harmonics: the BP major triad
# 3:5:7 is steps 0, 6 and 10. The Lambda mode picks 9 of the 13 steps, 0 1 3 4 6 7 9 10 12, which is exactly
# floor(3d/2) for d = 0..8: lam(d). The melody is a Lissajous figure in the same odd ratios, sines of 3, 5 and 7 turns
# a stage, so the generator and the tuning come from one idea. The timbre has odd partials only (a clarinet's), the
# spectrum the scale was made for. The meter is 12/8: four dotted beats of three eighths, an eighth floor(3t).
# Sources: H. Bohlen (1978), J. R. Pierce (1984), M. V. Mathews and J. R. Pierce on BP harmony (1988).

== species
name tritave
title the Bohlen-Pierce scale in 12/8: thirteen steps to the tritave, no octave, odd harmonics only
tuning Bohlen-Pierce, bp(k) = Tk + 12 k log2(3) / 13, through the Lambda mode lam(d) = floor(3d/2)
meter 12/8 (four dotted beats of three eighths)
generator a Lissajous figure in the odd ratios 3:5:7
form loop arcs
timbre odd partials only (clarinet-like)
bpm 76 92
weight 1
explore yes
register no
open pads+hat | bass+kick | lead | counter+perc | bass+lead

== helpers
tq(i) = mod(floor(Tn / 10^(7 - mod(i, 8))), 10)
tw(a, b, u) = a + (b - a) smooth((u - 0.6) / 0.4)

== frame
bp(k) = Tk + 12 k log2(3) / 13
lam(d) = 13 floor(d / 9) + floor(3 mod(d, 9) / 2)
