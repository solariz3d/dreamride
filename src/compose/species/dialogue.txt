# SPECIES: DIALOGUE. Call and response between two curves, on a breathing clock, over written harmony (src/compose/book.rs).
#
# Two lines talk. The lead calls for two bars (a voice, its vibrato arriving late, the lead that was loved over stillness);
# the counter-line answers for the next two with the call turned upside down (the inversion about degree 7, played a
# call-length later), or echoes it, or a falling answer resolves a rising question. The melodies are off the grid:
# every melodic voice reads a rubato clock v = t + 0.32 sin(tau t / 8), whose tempo breathes between 0.75 and 1.25 of
# the beat over each two bars and comes back to the grid at every seam (v(32) = 32). Since 2026-11-03 the harmony is a
# written progression (harmony.txt) in the song's key, and it stays on the grid (the bass and pads keep the bar while
# the voices breathe over it): the lines sing the pentatonic of its mode, pt(d) (minor or major pentatonic through the
# lens h), and land on a chord tone at each half bar of their own clock, ps(d, k, p). Sources: call and response in West
# African and African-American music (Floyd 1995); the rubato curve after Todd (1992) and Friberg and Sundberg (1999).

== species
name dialogue
title call and response between two curves, on a breathing clock, over written harmony
tuning 12-tone equal temperament: a written progression in the song's key, the voices on the pentatonic of its mode
meter 4/4 under rubato: v = t + 0.32 sin(tau t / 8), the tempo breathing 0.75 to 1.25
generator a call and its inversion, echo or answer, two bars each
harmony yes
form call and response inside loop arcs
timbre a voice lead with late vibrato, the pluck answering, brushes
bpm 76 92
weight 1.5
open lead | pads+lead | bass+hat | counter+perc | pads | bass+lead

== helpers
v = t + 0.32 sin(tau t / 8)
sw(a, b, u) = a + (b - a) smooth((u - 0.55) / 0.45)
ps(d, k, p) = if(mod(k, p) == 0, sc(pt(d), floor(k / p)), pt(d))
dw(p) = mod(p + 7, 12) - 34

== frame
pt(d) = h(7 floor(d / 5) + mod(floor(if(c < 4, 64320, 54210) / 10^mod(d, 5)), 10))

# ================================================================================================= calls (the lead)

== dialogue/lead.call | lead | energy 1 | density 1 | mood voice longing | glide
# the call: two bars of a falling and returning curve on eighths of the breathing clock, sliding into each note, the vibrato arriving late; silent while it is answered
Dc(k) = floor(7 + 2.5 sin(tau k / 11) - 2 frac(k / 8) + 1.5 cos(tau k / 5))
Dl = sw(ps(Dc(floor(2v)), floor(2v), 4), ps(Dc(floor(2v) + 1), floor(2v) + 1, 4), frac(2v))

voice lead {
  wave = additive
  partials = 0, -16, -4.5, -15, -11, -18.4, -16.1, -22.9, -22.9, -28
  rolloff = 660
  pitch = Dl + vibrato(note_age, 0.45, 0.5, 0.28, 5.2)
  gate = mod(v, 16) < 7.6
  gain = 0.19
  pan = 0.1
  attack = 60
  decay = 600
  sustain = 0.75
  release = 400
  reverb = 0.45
  delay = 0.2
}

== dialogue/lead.question | lead | energy 2 | density 2 | mood bright longing | glide
# a question: two bars climbing the pentatonic by eighths and stopping high, unresolved, sliding between the steps
Dc(k) = 4 + floor(mod(k, 16) / 2.2) + (mod(k, 3) == 2)
Dl = sw(ps(Dc(floor(2v)), floor(2v), 4), ps(Dc(floor(2v) + 1), floor(2v) + 1, 4), frac(2v))

voice lead {

  pan = 0.1
  attack = 25

  reverb = 0.4
  delay = 0.25
}

== dialogue/bass.breath | bass | energy 1 | density 1 | mood warm calm | glide

  pitch = Dz
  gate = 0.94 - frac(t / 2)
  gain = 0.17
  attack = 15
  decay = 600
  sustain = 0.65
  release = 150
}

== dialogue/bass.walk | bass | energy 2 | density 2 | mood groove warm
# a walk on the beats under the breathing voices: the bass note, the third, the fifth (a new chord at the half bar takes its own bass note), a ghost an octave up on the and of 2, and a half step under the next bar's bass on 4
Dz = if(mod(floor(t), 4) == 3, dw(Hr(2j + 2)) - 1, sc(dw(Hr(floor(t / 2))) + 3.5 mod(floor(t), 4) (Hr(floor(t / 2)) == Hr(2j)), floor(t / 2))) + 12 (frac(t) >= 0.5)

voice bass {
  wave = additive
  partials = -11, 0, -26, -12, -28, -20, -32, -21
  rolloff = 660
  pitch = Dz
  gate = (0.45 - frac(t)) + (mod(t, 4) >= 1.5)(mod(t, 4) < 1.7)

  attack = 4
  decay = 300
  sustain = 0.5
  release = 80
}
