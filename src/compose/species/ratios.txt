# SPECIES: RATIOS. Just intonation in 3/4: the harmony is the ratios (src/compose/book.rs, docs/composer.md "Species").
#
# Every pitch is a whole-number ratio of the tonic, sounded through J(r) = Jz + 12 log2(r): the bar's fundamental is a
# ratio Q(i) (numerator and denominator stored digit by digit), its chord is harmonics 4:5:6:7 of a fundamental two
# octaves down (the 7th harmonic is the interval no piano has), or the undertone chord 1/4:1/5:1/6. Leads climb the
# harmonic series of the bar's fundamental. The meter is 3/4: three pulses of 4/3 beat in each four-beat bar, so a
# pulse is floor(3t/4) and an eighth floor(3t/2). Timbres are pure sines and glass bells (few partials), so the just
# intervals beat against nothing. Sources: Partch (Genesis of a Music), Sethares (Tuning, Timbre, Spectrum, Scale).

== species
name ratios
title just intonation in 3/4: every pitch a whole-number ratio, chords from the harmonic series
tuning just intonation, J(r) = Jz + 12 log2(r)
meter 3/4 (three pulses in each four-beat bar)
generator the harmonic series of each bar's fundamental
form loop arcs
timbre pure sines and glass bells
bpm 80 100
weight 1
explore yes
open pads | bass+perc | lead | pads+kick | counter+hat | bass+lead

== helpers
dg(x, i) = mod(floor(x / 10^(7 - mod(i, 8))), 10)
gz(a, b, u) = a + (b - a) smooth((u - 0.7) / 0.3)
Q(i) = dg(Qn, i) / dg(Qd, i)

== frame
J(r) = Jz + 12 log2(r)

# ================================================================================================= leads

== ratios/lead.alphorn | lead | energy 2 | density 2 | mood wander bright | glide
# an alphorn: harmonics 6 to 16 of the bar's fundamental (11 and 13 sound between the keys), on eighths, chosen by two sines of periods 18 and 7, gliding the last 30% of each eighth
Jh(k) = clamp(floor(11 + 3.5 sin(tau k / 18) + 2.5 sin(tau k / 7)), 6, 16)
Jl = gz(J(Q(j) Jh(floor(1.5t)) / 2), J(Q(floor((t + 0.6667) / 4)) Jh(floor(1.5t) + 1) / 2), frac(1.5t))

voice lead {
  wave = additive
  partials = 0, -14, -22, -30
  rolloff = 660
  pitch = Jl
  gate = mod(floor(1.5t), 12) < 10
  gain = 0.2
  pan = 0.1
  attack = 30
  decay = 400
  sustain = 0.7
  release = 200
  reverb = 0.4
  delay = 0.2
}

== ratios/lead.overtones | lead | energy 1 | density 1 | mood voice calm | glide
# a voice climbing the overtone scale and coming back down, harmonics 10 to 16, one a pulse: 16 - |k mod 12 - 6|, sliding into each, the vibrato arriving late
Jh(k) = 16 - abs(mod(k, 12) - 6)
Jl = gz(J(Q(j) Jh(floor(0.75t)) / 2), J(Q(floor((t + 1.3333) / 4)) Jh(floor(0.75t) + 1) / 2), frac(0.75t))

voice lead {
  wave = additive
  partials = 0, -18, -26, -34
  rolloff = 660
  pitch = Jl + vibrato(note_age, 0.5, 0.6, 0.22, 5.2)
  gate = frac(t / 16) < 0.92
  gain = 0.2
  pan = 0.05
  attack = 80
  decay = 600
  sustain = 0.75
  release = 400
  reverb = 0.5
  delay = 0.2
}
