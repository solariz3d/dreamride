# SPECIES: MAQAM (the quarter-tone bounce, behind the Explore option since 2026-11-03). Physical models in quarter tones, half time (src/compose/book.rs).
#
# Phrases are shaped by bodies, not by bars. A ball dropped at the start of each two-bar phrase bounces with
# restitution e, each gap e times the last, so the bounces speed up and the phrase ends where the ball comes to rest:
# with the first gap 8(1 - e) the whole fall takes exactly the 8 beats of the phrase, and the bounce under position u is
# bk(u, e) = floor(log2(1 - u/8) / log2(e)), its phase bph(u, e). A note on every bounce, a kick on every bounce: the
# accelerando is the physics. A pendulum, cos(tau t / 4) damped by 2^(-t/6), swings the lead between two notes without
# steps; a damped spring, 2^(-12 u) cos(3 tau u), makes each note overshoot and settle. The tuning is 24-tone equal
# temperament (quarter tones), qt(k) = Mk + k/2, through maqam modes (Rast, Bayati, Hijaz) stored two digits a step,
# with neutral thirds between major and minor. Tempo 120-140 felt in half time. Sources: classical mechanics (the
# coefficient of restitution); on maqam, the Cairo Congress of Arab Music (1932) and S. Marcus (1989).

== species
name maqam
title maqam in quarter tones: phrases shaped by bouncing balls, pendulums and springs (the first bounce, kept for Explore)
tuning 24-tone equal temperament (quarter tones), qt(k) = Mk + k/2, through maqam modes
meter half time, with accelerandos made by the bounces
generator physical models: a bouncing ball, a damped pendulum, a damped spring
form loop arcs
timbre marimba-like bells and a round bass
bpm 120 140
weight 1
explore yes
open kick+bass | lead | pads+perc | hat+counter | bass+clap

== helpers
bk(u, e) = floor(log2(1 - min(u, 7.99) / 8) / log2(e))
bph(u, e) = (u - 8 + 8 e^bk(u, e)) / (8 (1 - e) e^bk(u, e))
mq(i) = mod(floor(Mn / 10^(7 - mod(i, 8))), 10)
Mu = mod(t, 8)

== frame
qt(k) = Mk + k / 2
rq(d) = 24 floor(d / 7) + mod(floor(Mr / 100^mod(d, 7)), 100)

== maqam/lead.ball | lead | energy 2 | density 2 | mood strange bright

voice lead {
  wave = additive
  partials = 0, -26, -10, -30, -16, -32
