# SPECIES: AUTOMATON. An elementary cellular automaton in 5/4 over written harmony (src/compose/book.rs).
#
# The generator is Wolfram's elementary cellular automaton: a row of ten cells (the ten eighths of a 5/4 bar), stored
# as the bits of an integer, stepped by Sx(x) = ca(Wr, x, 10). Each lead brings its rule (110 the one that computes,
# 90 the Sierpinski triangle, 30 Wolfram's chaos); without a lead, rule 45, an endless variation. Row n is
# Rw(n) = iter(Sx, Wz, n), one row a bar, counted across the whole stream by the stage clock S, so the automaton never
# restarts at a seam. The rhythm is the cells (cl), the melody is each cell's own neighbourhood code nb (the three bits
# the rule reads, 0 to 7) as a scale degree. Since 2026-11-03 the harmony is a written progression (harmony.txt) in
# the song's key: the melody is pulled to a chord tone at each half bar (and at the 3+2 accent), the arpeggio and pads
# turn the progression's voicing by the row's integer, and the basses play its bass notes. (It was whole tone, with no
# home; a whole-tone scale cannot hold a dominant resolving to a tonic.) The meter is 5/4: ten eighths in each
# four-beat bar, an eighth floor(5t/2). Sources: S. Wolfram, "Statistical mechanics of cellular automata", Rev. Mod.
# Phys. 55 (1983), and A New Kind of Science (2002) for rules 30 and 110; M. Cook (2004) on rule 110.

== species
name automaton
title an elementary cellular automaton in 5/4 on the whole-tone scale: the rule is the score
tuning 12-tone equal temperament: a written progression in the song's key, the melody on its mode through the lens h
meter 5/4 (ten eighths in each four-beat bar)
generator elementary cellular automata (rules 30, 110, 90, 45), one row a bar
harmony yes
form loop arcs
timbre square-ish chips, glass and dash percussion
bpm 84 104
weight 1
open kick+hat | lead | bass+perc | pads+hat | perc+clap | bass+lead

== helpers
Sx(x) = ca(Wr, x, 10)
Rw(n) = iter(Sx, Wz, n)
rn = 8 S + floor(t / 4)
cl(n, i) = mod(floor(Rw(n) / 2^mod(i, 10)), 2)
nb(n, i) = 4 cl(n, i + 1) + 2 cl(n, i) + cl(n, i + 9)
aw(p) = mod(p + 4, 12) - 40

== frame
S = 0
Wr = 45
Wz = 1

Wz = 33
Cz(k) = if((mod(k, 5) == 0) + (mod(k, 5) == 3), sc(h(3 + nb(8 S + floor(k / 5), 2 mod(k, 5))), 2 floor(k / 5) + (mod(k, 5) == 3)), h(3 + nb(8 S + floor(k / 5), 2 mod(k, 5))))
