# One fractal, the Takagi (blancmange) curve T, read at four time scales 4x apart: the song's sixteen-bar form, the phrase, the bar and the beat.
#
# T(u) sums tents at halving sizes, so every stretch of it looks like the whole. The chord roots are T across the whole
# song, one sample a bar: I ii iii IV IV V V V IV V V V IV IV iii ii, rising and falling with the curve. The lead is T at
# the phrase, the bar and the beat added together, so each beat's figure has the shape of the bar's, and the bar's of the
# phrase's; it glides into every next step. The kick lands wherever the lead falls by three degrees or more, and the hats
# read T across two beats as their loudness. The contour voice draws T across the whole song, unquantized until the
# glide lens g holds it on scale tones. Everything is the major scale lens h. 9 definitions and 4 time names.
# Measured (tools/measure/analyze.py): band energy 43/29/26/1.7/0.3 %, 110 spectral peaks per frame; the reference
# recordings measure 41/37/20/1.6/0.2 % with 108 (city) and 60/26/13/0.9/0.3 % with 143 (v).

bpm 96
beats 64
loop 1
reverb_size 0.7
reverb_predelay 18
delay_beats 0.75
delay_feedback 0.3
warmth 0.3

tri(v) = abs(v - floor(v + 0.5))
T(u) = tri(u) + tri(2u)/2 + tri(4u)/4 + tri(8u)/8 + tri(16u)/16 + tri(32u)/32
h(d) = floor((12d + 5) / 7)
g(u) = h(floor(u)) + (h(floor(u) + 1) - h(floor(u))) smooth((frac(u) - 0.75) / 0.25)
R(m) = floor(7 T(floor(m / 16) / 16))
L(m) = floor(6 T(m / 64) + 6 T(m / 16) + 4 T(m / 4))
P(m) = h(R(m) + L(m)) - 2
lead = P(n) + (P(n + 1) - P(n)) smooth((f - 0.6) / 0.4)
fall(m) = L(m - 1) - L(m) >= 3
n = floor(4t)
f = frac(4t)
b = frac(t)
j = floor(t / 4)

  wave = additive
  partials = 0, -16, -4.5, -15, -11, -18.4, -16.1, -22.9, -22.9, -28

}
