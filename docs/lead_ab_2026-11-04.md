# Lead A/B: the blancmange wanderer against a phrased lead (registered 2026-11-04, before listening)

The keeper, 2026-11-04: the generation system "was close to being decent, but is missing real musical feel, like made
by someone who is just pressing shit." The diagnosis, read from `src/compose/book.txt`: `lead.blancmange` holds its gate
open about 15.4 beats of every 16 (`gate = frac(t / 16) < 0.96`), moves pitch every eighth with a glide
(`L = gl(Lm(floor(2t)), ...)`), and makes every note fresh from a fractal of the step index. The line never rests,
never repeats an idea, and never arrives. Seven of the twelve leads keep a gate like it.

## What was built (outside the repo, nothing here changes the engine or the book)

One band, written by the engine's own `book::install` and `book::full`: `prog.royal_road`, `bass.walk`, `pads.struck`,
`kick.pickup`, `hat.takagi`, `clap.backbeat`; 64 beats at 106 bpm (two passes of the 8-bar progression).

- **A** carries `lead.blancmange` unchanged.
- **B** replaces only the lead's definitions and its `pitch`, `gate` and `amp` lines. The voice block is otherwise
  identical: same partials, gain, envelope, pan, reverb and delay. B is four 2-bar cells (a question, its answer, the
  question a step higher with a push, an answer that resolves to the root). It uses one rhythmic motif in every cell,
  rests between phrases, chord tones on strong beats, two notes anticipating the next chord by an eighth, and accents
  on the target notes.

Measured with `book::line_stats` on each lead (median matched on purpose: B was first 5 semitones higher, a confound,
and was lowered three scale steps before this registration):

## The blind test

The two renders were assigned to `clip_1.wav` and `clip_2.wav` (in `Downloads\dreamride_ab\`) by a coin flip
(`crypto.randomInt(2)`). The key was written to a file outside this repo and not shown to the writer either. Its digest:

    sha256 16dd4bfb374d74681ee54d3fa5621a69a0ca17b2aeaa3a738cf1efd42e244681

The listener names the clip that sounds more like music someone played, and may say "neither" or "same".

**Prediction:** the listener picks the clip that is B.
**Falsified if:** the listener picks A, or hears no difference worth naming.

**What a pass would not show:**
- B differs from A in articulation as well as phrasing (a scoop into each note and vibrato on long ones: glide share
  1.00 against 0.20), so a pick for B does not separate phrasing from articulation.
- It is one listener and one pair, over one progression.
- A pass says the direction is right, not that B is good.

## Result

2026-11-04, ~04:58. The keeper, blind: *"it is better the first one listening to it"*.

The key file, re-checked before opening: `sha256 16dd4bfb374d74681ee54d3fa5621a69a0ca17b2aeaa3a738cf1efd42e244681`, identical to the
digest above. Its contents: `clip_1 = B (phrased), clip_2 = A (lead.blancmange)`, nonce `143c7122315eeb3a`.

**The prediction held: the listener picked B.** Within the limits named above: one listener, one pair, phrasing not
separated from articulation. The direction is right; B itself is a hand-written test lead, not yet a generator.
